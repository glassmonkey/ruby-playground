Operators and precedence

Group calculations deliberately and inspect comparisons.

Learn

Operator precedence determines how an expression groups its parts. Multiplication groups before addition. Parentheses make a different grouping explicit and can also make your intent easier to read.

== compares values; = performs assignment. Many Ruby operators are method calls on an object, so their behavior depends on the receiver. For example, + adds numbers but joins strings.

Experiment

Replace (2 + 3) * 4 with 2 * (3 + 4).

Answer

The result becomes 14: the parenthesized addition is evaluated as a group, then multiplied by 2.
